Getting Started

The Ultimate Meal Planner helps you create a monthly meal calendar, save recipes, generate shopping lists, manage pantry items, print your calendar, and backup your data.

Start by adding recipes in Recipe Box. Then click a day on the calendar and assign meals from the left panel.

Monthly Calendar

  • Use the Month and Year controls to choose the calendar you want to view.
  • Click Today to return to the current month.
  • Click Previous or Next to move between months.
  • Click Save if you want to manually save the current month.
  • The app also auto-saves many calendar changes.

Assign Meals

  • Click any active calendar day to open the Assign Meals panel.
  • Drag a recipe from the panel onto a calendar day.
  • If a recipe fits more than one meal category, choose the correct meal slot when prompted.
  • Drag a calendar meal to the Trash box in the Assign Meals panel to remove it from the calendar.
  • Use Filter Meals to show or hide Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack/Dessert recipes.

Recipe Box

  • Open Recipe Box from the hamburger menu.
  • Click Add Recipe to create a new recipe.
  • Use Category Filter to view recipes by meal category.
  • Click a recipe card to view the full recipe.
  • Use Edit inside the recipe view to update an existing recipe.

Recipe Cards

  • Recipe Name is required.
  • Description, Prep Time, Cook Time, Total Time, Servings, Ingredients, Instructions, Notes, Nutrition, and Source URL are optional.
  • Enter one ingredient per line.
  • Description, Instructions, Notes, and Nutrition preserve line spacing in the recipe view.
  • Prep, Cook, and Total Time are shown in minutes.

Shopping List

  • Open Shopping List from the hamburger menu.
  • Click Generate to create a shopping list from the current calendar month.
  • Click Print to print the shopping list.
  • Click Share to share the shopping list when your device/browser supports native sharing.
  • Click Clear to remove the saved shopping list for the current month.

Pantry

  • Open Pantry from the hamburger menu.
  • Checked pantry items are treated as items you already have and are removed from generated shopping lists.
  • Unchecked pantry items appear as item-only with no measurements.
  • Add your own pantry item by typing it and clicking Add.
  • Drag a pantry item to the Trash box to delete it.
  • Pantry changes automatically update the saved shopping list for the current calendar month.

Conversion Chart

  • Open Conversion Chart from the hamburger menu.
  • Use it as a quick reference for common kitchen measurements.
  • The chart includes teaspoons, tablespoons, cups, pints, quarts, gallons, ounces, pounds, and common kitchen notes.

Printing

  • Use Print from the hamburger menu to print the monthly calendar.
  • The calendar is designed for one-page landscape printing.
  • Use Print inside the Shopping List window to print only the shopping list.

Sharing And Importing Recipes

  • Open a recipe from Recipe Box.
  • Click Share to share recipe text when supported by your device/browser.
  • Click Export to download a single recipe JSON file.
  • Use Import Recipe in Recipe Box to import a recipe JSON file.

Backup And Restore

  • Open Admin from the hamburger menu.
  • Click Export Full Backup to download a backup of your app data.
  • Click Import Backup to restore from a backup file.
  • Restoring a backup overwrites the current app database on that device.
  • Create a new full backup regularly, especially before wiping or moving data to another device.

Themes, Dark Mode, And Fonts

  • Open Theme from the hamburger menu to choose a color theme.
  • Use Dark Mode from the hamburger menu to switch between light and dark display modes.
  • Use Calendar Font to change the calendar title, month/year, and day number font.

Offline Use

  • The app is designed to work offline after it has loaded and the service worker is registered by the browser.
  • Your saved data is stored locally in the browser database on the device you are using.
  • Use Export Full Backup if you want a copy of your data outside the browser.

Wipe Database Safety

  • Wipe Database permanently clears app data stored on the current device/browser.
  • Always export a full backup first if you want to keep your data.
  • The app requires confirmation text before wiping the database.

Troubleshooting Tips

  • If sharing is not supported, the app may download a text or JSON file instead.
  • If a recipe does not appear in Assign Meals, check its meal category.
  • If a shopping list looks wrong, check the recipe ingredients and Pantry settings.
